NATIONALISM

An exploration of nationalism as an ideology, with a focus on India.

Introduction

What is Nationalism?

Nationalism is understood in many ways. This is one personal understanding.

To me nationalism is the thought and act of preferring one's own country, as defined by the modern state concept, over any other identity of an individual. In addition to the love for one's own nation, it means to keep your country over everything.

Key Ideas

Nation

A group of people who see themselves as sharing a common identity, such as history, language, culture, or a shared political life.

State

The political organisation that governs a territory, with its own laws, government, and borders. The "modern state concept" in the definition above refers to this.

National identity

A person's sense of belonging to a nation. Nationalism asks how that belonging compares with other identities, such as religion, caste, language, or region.

The Indian Context

Indian Nationalism

Modern Indian nationalism took shape under British colonial rule. Thinkers and political movements disagreed about what the Indian nation was and how to achieve freedom. Some stressed non-violence, others revolution; some a shared civilisation, others a modern, secular state.

This website looks at ten figures whose ideas and actions help explain that debate.
