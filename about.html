About This Project

Why this website exists

Many people are unaware of even the basic ideas surrounding nationalism. This website is a simple, beginner-friendly introduction to the subject.

A personal introduction

The definition of nationalism on the Home page is the creator's own understanding. It is not presented as the single, universally accepted definition. Nationalism is understood in many ways.

The Indian context

The website focuses mainly on India. It introduces ten figures whose ideas or actions help in understanding Indian nationalism.

Personal view and historical record

The figure profiles are meant to be based on documented sources, and each profile will list its sources. They are kept separate from the creator's own perspective. The ten figures did not all hold the same views, and where they differ, the profiles aim to show it.
